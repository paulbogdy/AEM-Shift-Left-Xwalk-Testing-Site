How do I reset my password?

Go to the Velox login page at app.veloxhq.com and click "Forgot your password?" below the sign-in form. Enter your email address and we'll send you a reset link within a few minutes. If you don't receive it, check your spam folder or contact support at support@veloxhq.com.

How do I add team members to my workspace?

From your Velox dashboard, navigate to Settings → Members and click "Invite Members." Enter email addresses (comma-separated for multiple), choose a role (Admin, Member, or Guest), and click Send Invites. Invitees will receive an email with instructions to join. You can manage roles and remove members from the same page.

How do I connect integrations?

Go to Settings → Integrations and browse the available integrations by category or search by name. Click an integration to see setup instructions — most require authorizing Velox to access your other account via OAuth. Once connected, integrations are available as trigger and action options when building automations.

What is your data retention policy?

Active account data is retained indefinitely while your subscription is active. When you cancel, your data is retained for 30 days (Starter), 90 days (Pro), or 12 months (Enterprise) before permanent deletion — giving you time to export. You can export all your data at any time from Settings → Export. We never sell or share your data with third parties.

How do I export my data?

Go to Settings → Export and choose the format (CSV, JSON, or PDF for reports). You can export individual projects, specific data types (tasks, comments, automations logs), or your full workspace. Large exports are processed in the background and emailed to you as a download link when ready, typically within 30 minutes.

How do I cancel my subscription?

You can cancel anytime from Settings → Billing → Cancel Subscription. Your access continues until the end of your current billing period. There are no cancellation fees. If you cancel an annual plan, you won't be charged at the next renewal but you won't receive a prorated refund for unused months. Contact billing@veloxhq.com if you have billing questions.
