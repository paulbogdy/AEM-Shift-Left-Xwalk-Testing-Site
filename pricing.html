Can I try Velox before buying?

Yes — every plan starts with a free 14-day trial that includes all Pro features. No credit card required to start. At the end of the trial you can choose a paid plan or continue on the free Starter tier (limited to 3 users and 3 workflows).

What happens when my trial ends?

When your 14-day trial ends, your account automatically moves to the free Starter tier. Your data is preserved. Automations that exceed Starter limits will be paused (not deleted) until you upgrade. You'll receive email reminders before the trial ends so you can plan ahead.

Can I change plans at any time?

Yes. You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time from your account settings. Upgrades take effect immediately and you'll be charged a prorated amount. Downgrades take effect at the end of your current billing period. Annual plans can be downgraded at renewal time.

Is there a discount for annual billing?

Yes — paying annually saves you 20% compared to monthly billing. Starter drops from $29/mo to $23/mo, and Pro drops from $79/mo to $63/mo. Enterprise pricing is custom and always includes annual billing. You can switch to annual billing at any time from your account settings.

How does per-user pricing work?

Plans are priced per workspace (not per user) up to the included seat limit. Starter includes up to 5 users. Pro includes up to 25 users. If you need more seats on Pro, additional users are $8/user/month. Enterprise pricing is custom and includes unlimited users. Guests (external collaborators) are always free on all plans.
