Our story

Velox was founded in 2019 by three operators who'd spent a combined 30 years running teams at high-growth startups. They were frustrated by the same problem: even with great tools, teams were spending more time managing their work than doing it. Every handoff required a Slack message. Every status update meant a meeting. Every report took hours to compile manually.

The original Velox prototype was built in a weekend to automate a single workflow — routing support tickets to the right team. Within six months, it had grown into a full platform used by 200 beta teams. By the end of 2020, Velox had raised $12M Series A and launched publicly.

Today, over 12,000 teams in 80 countries use Velox to run everything from engineering sprints to customer onboarding to financial reporting. Our mission remains the same: give every team the tools to do their best work, without the friction.

Life at Velox

We're a remote-first team of 140 people spread across 20 countries. We believe great talent exists everywhere, and we've built a culture that makes distributed work feel seamless — not like a compromise.

We come together for quarterly offsites, ship weeks, and team celebrations. Between those moments, we rely on async-friendly workflows (yes, we use Velox internally), clear documentation, and a deep respect for focus time.

Benefits include competitive equity, unlimited PTO, a home office stipend, learning & development budget, and full health coverage. We're committed to building a team that reflects the diversity of the people we serve.
