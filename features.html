How does workflow automation work in Velox?

Velox automation is built on a trigger-condition-action model. You choose what event starts the workflow (a trigger), define optional conditions to filter when it runs, and specify one or more actions to execute. Triggers can be anything from a task status change to a calendar date, a webhook, or a form submission. Actions include updating records, sending notifications, creating tasks, calling external APIs, and more.

Are there limits on the number of automations I can create?

Starter plans include up to 10 active automations with 1,000 monthly automation runs. Pro plans include unlimited automations and 50,000 monthly runs. Enterprise plans have no limits on automations or runs, with priority processing to ensure critical workflows execute immediately.

Can I customize the views and dashboards?

Yes. Every workspace in Velox supports multiple view types: Kanban board, list, table, calendar, timeline (Gantt), and map views. You can save custom views with filters, groupings, and sort orders. Analytics dashboards are fully customizable — add any widget, choose your metrics, and arrange them to match your reporting needs.

How does guest access work?

Guests are external users (clients, contractors, partners) who can be invited into specific projects or workspaces without accessing your full Velox organization. Guests are free on all plans and have permission levels you control: view only, comment, or full edit access on the specific content they're invited to.

Does Velox support mobile?

Velox has native iOS and Android apps with full feature parity for the most common workflows. Create and update tasks, leave comments, review notifications, and run reports from your phone. The mobile apps work offline and sync automatically when you reconnect.
